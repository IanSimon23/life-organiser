# The Life Organiser: Filling It In and Keeping It Current

**What this is:** the second of two guides. The first guide (*Setup Guide*) built the empty system. This one tells you what to put in it, where each thing goes, and how to keep it up to date.

**When to use it:**
- **The first time:** work through Parts A to F in order. Allow a few sessions over a week or two. It doesn't need doing in one go.
- **Every six months:** use the **Six-Monthly Review** at the end. It takes about 30 minutes.

---

## Where everything goes

Everything you record ends up in one of four places. When in doubt, use this table.

| Place | What goes there | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **The Vault** | Passwords, PINs, account numbers, **and scans of anything a thief could use**: passport, driving licence, bank statements, pension statements | Locked behind your master password |
| **Proton Drive** (`Life Organiser/Documents` folder) | Scans of lower-risk papers: will, power of attorney, insurance schedules, guarantees, funeral plan | Safe online, easy for your executor to read |
| **The Overview** | *What* exists, *who* to contact, *where* the originals are. No secrets. | It's the first thing your executor reads |
| **The Document Box** (physical) | The original paper documents | Some originals are legally needed. A scan is not enough. |

**The quick test:** *"If a stranger found this scan, could they pretend to be me or take money?"*
**Yes** → the Vault. **No** → Proton Drive.

## Part A: Get the folders ready

In Proton Drive, inside your `Life Organiser` folder, create a folder called `Documents`. Inside that, create:

- `01 Legal`
- `02 Property`
- `03 Insurance`
- `04 Vehicle`
- `05 Funeral and Wishes`
- `06 Other`

Your `Life Organiser` folder should now contain:

```
Life Organiser/
├── Overview
├── Life Organiser Vault
└── Documents/
    ├── 01 Legal
    ├── 02 Property
    ├── 03 Insurance
    ├── 04 Vehicle
    ├── 05 Funeral and Wishes
    └── 06 Other
```

## Part C: Scan your documents

### How to scan
**Option 1: a home scanner or printer-scanner.** Save straight into the right Proton Drive folder.

**Option 2: your phone.**
- iPhone: open the **Notes** app → new note → camera icon → **Scan Documents**.
- Android: open the **Google Drive** app → **+** → **Scan**.

Then save or share the scan to your computer and put it in the right place.

> ⚠️ **After using your phone, delete the scan from it**, including from "Recently Deleted". Your phone may back up to the cloud automatically, and your passport shouldn't end up there.

### Settings
- Save as **PDF**.
- Scan **every page**, including the backs if anything is written on them.
- Check each scan is readable before moving on.

### Naming
Name every file the same way, so things are easy to find and you can see at a glance how old they are:

**`Year-Month What it is`**

For example:
- `2026-11 Will.pdf`
- `2026-11 Passport.pdf`
- `2026-11 Nationwide statement.pdf`
- `2019-03 Boiler guarantee.pdf`

Use the date **on the document**, not today's date.

### Putting scans in the Vault
1. Open the Vault in KeePassXC.
2. Find the right entry (for example the entry for that bank), or create one.
3. Open the entry → **Advanced** → **Attachments** → **Add**, and choose the scan.
4. Click **OK** and save the Vault (Ctrl+S, or Cmd+S on a Mac).
5. **Delete the original scan file** from your computer. It's now safely inside the Vault. (Check your computer's Recycle Bin/Bin too.)

## Part D: Fill in the Vault

Make one entry for each account, device or service. Go group by group, and don't try to do it all at once.

### What a good entry looks like
| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Nationwide current account |
| **Username** | Customer number or email address |
| **Password** | The password (or PIN, clearly labelled) |
| **URL** | nationwide.co.uk |
| **Notes** | Sort code and account number. Phone number. Memorable information. *"Two-step login code goes to my mobile."* Anything your executor should know. |
| **Attachments** | Recent statement scan |

### Work through the groups
- ☐ **01 Devices:** phone passcode, computer password, tablet, Wi-Fi password, alarm code
- ☐ **02 Email & Phone:** every email account; mobile phone account (whoever controls the phone number can often reset everything else)
- ☐ **03 Money:** banks, savings, ISAs, pensions, investments, PayPal, credit cards
- ☐ **04 Home & Property:** utilities, mortgage, council account, home insurance login
- ☐ **05 Insurance:** logins for each policy
- ☐ **06 Digital Life:** photo storage, social media, Amazon, streaming services, subscriptions
- ☐ **07 Security Codes:** backup codes for every account that has two-step login

**A good tip:** for every account with two-step login, check that the backup codes are saved in group 07. Without them, your executor can't get in even with the password.

## Part E: Fill in the Overview

The Overview is written for someone who is upset and doesn't know where to start. Write it as if you're speaking to them. Use the headings from the Setup Guide and fill each one in.

- **Read me first.** The first few things to do, in order. For example: *"1. Call [solicitor]. 2. The Envelope is at [place]. 3. Register the death, and ask the registrar about the Tell Us Once service."*
- **Key people:** names and phone numbers for your executor, solicitor, accountant, GP, close family and anyone else to tell.
- **Where things are:** where the original will is, where the Document Box is, where the Envelope is.
- **Money:** list every bank, pension, investment and policy *by name only*, for example *"Nationwide: current account and ISA."* No numbers. They're in the Vault.
- **Property, insurance and regular bills:** who supplies what, and which bills go out by direct debit. A simple table works best. The last two columns are optional, but they make the *Money MOT* much easier:

  | Provider | What for | How paid | Monthly cost *(optional)* | Renewal / contract end *(optional)* |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | e.g. BT | Broadband | Direct debit | £ | Month and year |
  | e.g. Aviva | Home insurance | Annual, card | £ | Month and year |

  Include subscriptions too (streaming, memberships, apps).
- **Digital life:** which accounts exist, and what you'd like done with each (close it, keep it, memorialise it, download the photos first).
- **My wishes:** funeral (burial or cremation, music, religious or not), who gets personal items, any messages.
- **Last updated:** today's date.

**Check before saving:** read it through once looking only for passwords, PINs or account numbers. If you find any, move them to the Vault.

## Part F: Store the originals

### Set up a Document Box
Get **one** fireproof and waterproof document box or bag (these are fairly cheap). Everything marked *"Document Box"* in Part B goes in it.

- Keep it in **one fixed place** at home, and tell your executor where.
- Put the documents in clear plastic wallets, in the same order as the folders (Legal, Property, Insurance and so on).
- On the inside of the lid, tape a list of what's inside.
- **Never** put the Envelope from the Setup Guide in this box.

### Anything held elsewhere
Some originals may be held by your solicitor (often the will) or your mortgage lender (sometimes the deeds). That's fine. Just write down **who holds each one** in the Overview under *Where things are*.

### Shredding
Anything marked *"Shred after scanning"* can be shredded once the scan is saved and checked. Don't keep piles of old statements. They're an identity-theft risk, and the Vault has what's needed.

### Finish the first round
- ☐ Print the Overview and give it to your executor.
- ☐ If you chose to share Proton access immediately (Setup Guide Step 7, Option A), make sure your executor has the login details.
- ☐ Fill in the first line of the Review Log (at the end of this guide).

## Six-Monthly Review

Pick two easy-to-remember dates, for example **when the clocks change** in March and October. Set a reminder in your phone calendar.

Allow about 30 minutes, and work through this list.

### 1. What's changed? (10 minutes)
Think back over the last six months. Have you:
- ☐ Opened or closed a bank, savings or pension account?
- ☐ Changed any passwords, PINs or phone numbers?
- ☐ Started or cancelled a policy, subscription or direct debit?
- ☐ Bought or sold property, a car, or anything valuable?
- ☐ Signed anything new (a will, power of attorney, guarantee)?
- ☐ Had any change in family or key contacts (new solicitor, new address)?

For each "yes": update the Vault, the Overview, the Drive folders and the Document Box as needed, following Parts B to F.

### 2. Refresh the scans (10 minutes)
- ☐ Scan **one new statement** per bank and pension, and swap it for the old attachment in the Vault.
- ☐ Check for anything that has expired or been renewed (passport, driving licence, insurance schedules), and scan the new versions.
- ☐ Delete any old scans that are no longer relevant.

### 3. Check it all works (5 minutes)
- ☐ Open the Vault. Do you still remember the master password without looking?
- ☐ Pick **two entries at random** and check those logins still work.
- ☐ Read the Overview top to bottom. Would it make sense to someone who's never seen it?

### 4. Hand over (5 minutes)
- ☐ Update the "Last updated" date in the Overview.
- ☐ Print a fresh Overview for your executor, and ask them to shred the old one.
- ☐ **Only if** you changed the master password, Proton password or phone passcode: write a new Envelope (Setup Guide, Step 6).
- ☐ Fill in the Review Log below.

---

**Optional, same day:** while everything is fresh, work through the **Money MOT** guide to check you're not overpaying and your savings are earning a decent rate.
